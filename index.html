Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to what most clients ask before reaching out.

Do you work with international clients?
Yes — I have worked with clients in Canada, the US, and Australia. I am comfortable with async communication, different time zones, and tools like Slack, Zoom, Jira, and Upwork. Remote collaboration is my standard mode of work.
How quickly can you start on a project?
I am currently available and can typically start within 1–3 business days after our initial discovery call and agreement on scope. For urgent production issues, I can often respond same-day.
What does a typical engagement look like?
Most projects start with a discovery call and a scoped proposal. I offer both fixed-price project engagements (e.g., a specific migration or optimization task) and ongoing retainer-based consulting for continuous DBA support, reporting, or data engineering work.
Do you only work with SQL Server, or other databases too?
My primary expertise is MS SQL Server, but I have hands-on experience with MySQL, PostgreSQL, and Google BigQuery. I have also completed database migrations across all of these platforms, so I understand the differences deeply — not just theoretically.
Can you help with an ongoing production issue right now?
Absolutely. Production emergencies (deadlocks, crashes, slow queries, data corruption) are something I handle regularly. Reach out via email or LinkedIn with a brief description of your issue and I will respond as quickly as possible.
Will you also help my internal team understand the changes?
Yes — knowledge transfer is part of every engagement. I document all changes, explain the rationale in plain language, and offer a handover session so your internal team is empowered, not dependent. I want to make your team stronger, not create a dependency on me.
